# Postwel Privacy Policy

*Last updated: July 2026*

Postwel ("we," "our," or "the app") is operated by Gracen Studio. This
policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it's
protected.

## Information we collect

- **Account information**: your email address and password (stored securely
  via Supabase Auth; we never see or store your password in plain text).
- **Brand foundation details**: information you provide during onboarding,
  such as your name, role, industry, target audience, and brand voice.
- **LinkedIn account information**: when you connect your LinkedIn account,
  we receive and store your LinkedIn profile name, photo, and an access
  token that lets Postwel publish posts on your behalf, with your explicit
  permission. We do not receive your LinkedIn password.
- **Content you create**: posts you write and publish through Postwel, and
  engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) associated with those posts.

## How we use this information

- To operate your account and personalize the content Postwel helps you
  create.
- To publish posts to LinkedIn strictly at your direction, using the
  permissions you grant when connecting your account.
- To show you engagement metrics for posts you've published.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data to train third-party
advertising products.

## How we protect your information

- All data is stored in a Supabase project with row-level security, meaning
  your data is only ever accessible to your own account.
- LinkedIn access tokens are stored securely and are only used to take
  actions you explicitly request (connecting your account or publishing a
  post).

## Your controls

You can disconnect your LinkedIn account or delete your Postwel account at
any time. Disconnecting revokes Postwel's ability to post on your behalf.

## Third parties

Postwel uses LinkedIn's own API, directly, to publish content and manage
your connection — we do not route your data through unrelated third-party
services.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Gracen Studio.
